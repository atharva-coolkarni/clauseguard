RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Bengaluru on 22 January 2026 between Divya Iyer, residing at 25, Park Avenue, Whitefield, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Nikhil Reddy, presently residing at 91, Main Road, Behala, Kolkata (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. D-404, Lakeview Towers, Whitefield, Bengaluru (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 February 2026 to 31 December 2026.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 25,500, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Tenant has paid the Landlord an interest-free, refundable security deposit of Rs. 25,500, equal to one month's rent. The Landlord shall refund the deposit on the date the Tenant hands over vacant possession of the Premises, after deducting only rent or utility charges that remain unpaid on that date.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. Normal wear and tear arising from ordinary residential use, including fading or minor marks on painted walls, shall not be treated as damage and shall not be charged to the Tenant at the end of the tenancy.

8. During the last month of the tenancy, the Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants after giving 24 hours' notice, at times mutually convenient to both parties.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Bengaluru.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
