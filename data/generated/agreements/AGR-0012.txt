RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Kochi on 24 June 2026 between Joseph Shetty, residing at 71, Main Road, Kakkanad, Kochi (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Aditya Mehta, presently residing at 183, Temple Street, Sector 50, Noida (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 1168, Maple Heights, Kaloor, Kochi (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of twelve months commencing on 1 July 2026 and ending on 30 June 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 18,000, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Tenant shall keep with the Landlord a refundable deposit of Rs. 18,000, being one month's rent. The Landlord shall repay it within 15 days after the Tenant moves out, and shall give the Tenant a written list of any amounts deducted along with the supporting bills.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. Any damage to the Premises or its fittings caused by the Tenant, the Tenant's family members or visitors shall be repaired at the Tenant's cost, except ordinary wear and tear.

8. Throughout the tenancy, the Landlord shall be entitled to show the Premises to anyone interested in renting or buying it, at any time, and the Tenant shall raise no objection.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Kerala.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
