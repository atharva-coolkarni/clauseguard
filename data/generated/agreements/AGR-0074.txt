RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Mumbai on 29 March 2026 between Rohan Patel, residing at 7, Temple Street, Malad West, Mumbai (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Kavya Banerjee, presently residing at 57, MG Road, Sector 137, Noida (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 896, Ganga Residency, Goregaon East, Mumbai (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 April 2026 and ending on 28 February 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 32,000 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 64,000 may be adjusted only against (a) unpaid rent, (b) unpaid electricity, water or maintenance bills, and (c) the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Both parties shall record the condition of the Premises with dated photographs at move-in and move-out.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. A fixed amount of Rs. 12,000 towards painting and deep cleaning shall be deducted from the security deposit when the Tenant vacates, whether or not any painting or cleaning is required.

8. The Landlord may inspect the Premises at any time after informing the Tenant by phone or message at least 4 hour(s) in advance.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Maharashtra.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
