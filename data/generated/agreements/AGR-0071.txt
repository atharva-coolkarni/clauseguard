RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Kochi on 21 November 2026 between Meera Sharma, residing at 12, Station Road, Palarivattom, Kochi (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Aditya Sharma, presently residing at 106, Park Avenue, Ballygunge, Kolkata (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. 392 in Shanti Enclave, Edappally, Kochi - 682068, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 December 2026 and ending on 31 October 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 14,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. A refundable security deposit of Rs. 14,500 (one month's rent) is payable on signing. Within 15 days of the Tenant vacating, the Landlord shall return the deposit together with an itemised statement of any deductions, supported by bills or receipts.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Landlord from entering the Premises without notice where it is necessary to prevent immediate danger to life or serious damage to the building, provided the Tenant is informed at once.

8. On vacating, the Tenant shall remove all personal belongings and rubbish and leave the Premises broom-clean, with all keys returned to the Landlord.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Kerala.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
