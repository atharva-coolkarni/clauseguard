RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Chennai on 28 September 2026 between Rohan Nair, residing at 34, Park Avenue, T. Nagar, Chennai (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Nikhil Verma, presently residing at 118, Temple Street, Andheri West, Mumbai (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 589, Orchid Court, Adyar, Chennai (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 October 2026 and ending on 31 August 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 47,500, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 47,500 shall be refunded to the Tenant at the sole discretion of the Landlord after the Tenant vacates the Premises, subject to such deductions as the Landlord may consider appropriate.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Tenant shall hand over the Premises in brand-new condition, failing which the Landlord shall be entitled to recover the cost of restoring the Premises to such condition from the Tenant.

8. During the last month of the tenancy, the Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants after giving 24 hours' notice, at times mutually convenient to both parties.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Chennai.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
