RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Bengaluru on 16 June 2026 between Joseph Joshi, residing at 245, Main Road, Koramangala, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Divya Menon, presently residing at 225, Main Road, Goregaon East, Mumbai (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. A-901 in Orchid Court, Koramangala, Bengaluru - 560074, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 July 2026 and ending on 31 May 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 25,000, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. No deduction shall be made from the deposit of Rs. 50,000 except for rent arrears, pending utility or society maintenance bills, and the reasonable repair cost of damage caused by the Tenant, excluding ordinary wear and tear. Dated photographs of the Premises shall be taken by both parties when the Tenant moves in and when the Tenant moves out.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. Any damage to the Premises or its fittings caused by the Tenant, the Tenant's family members or visitors shall be repaired at the Tenant's cost, except ordinary wear and tear.

8. The Landlord shall not enter the Premises without at least 24 hours' notice to the Tenant, except in an emergency such as a fire, a gas leak or a serious water leak, in which case the Landlord may enter at once but must inform the Tenant immediately.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Bengaluru.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
