RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Kolkata, West Bengal on 24 October 2026 by and between Fatima Fernandes (the "Landlord") of the one part and Deepa Singh (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 728, Maple Heights, Salt Lake, Kolkata (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of twelve months commencing on 1 November 2026 and ending on 31 October 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 36,500 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. If the Tenant vacates the Premises before the expiry of the term for any reason whatsoever, including after giving notice, the entire security deposit of Rs. 73,000 shall stand forfeited to the Landlord.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. During the last month of the tenancy, the Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants after giving 24 hours' notice, at times mutually convenient to both parties.

8. The Tenant shall return the Premises in a reasonably clean condition, similar to its condition at the start of the tenancy, normal wear and tear excepted. The Tenant shall not be required to pay for professional deep cleaning or repainting.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Kolkata.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
