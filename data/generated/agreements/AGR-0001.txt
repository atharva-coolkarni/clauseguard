RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Bengaluru on 19 November 2026 between Ravi Khan, residing at 144, Station Road, Jayanagar, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Divya Singh, presently residing at 108, Station Road, Anna Nagar, Chennai (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. A-101 in Silver Oak Apartments, Jayanagar, Bengaluru - 560012, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 December 2026 to 31 October 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 51,000, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 51,000 shall be refunded to the Tenant at the sole discretion of the Landlord after the Tenant vacates the Premises, subject to such deductions as the Landlord may consider appropriate.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Landlord may inspect the Premises at any time after informing the Tenant by phone or message at least 4 hour(s) in advance.

8. At the time of vacating the Premises, the Tenant shall get the entire Premises repainted at the Tenant's own cost, irrespective of the condition of the walls or the period of occupation.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Karnataka.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
