RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Chennai, Tamil Nadu on 29 August 2026 by and between Aditya Chatterjee (the "Landlord") of the one part and Rahul Nair (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 522, Shanti Enclave, Adyar, Chennai (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 September 2026 and ending on 31 July 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 48,000, payable in advance on or before the 5th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Landlord shall be entitled to deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 96,000 any rent or utility dues outstanding on the date of vacating, and the cost of repairing any damage caused by the Tenant, as shown by bills which the Landlord shall share with the Tenant before making the deduction.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. The Landlord or his authorised agents shall have the right to enter the Premises at any time, with or without notice, for the purpose of inspection.

8. On vacating, the Tenant shall remove all personal belongings and rubbish and leave the Premises broom-clean, with all keys returned to the Landlord.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Chennai.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
