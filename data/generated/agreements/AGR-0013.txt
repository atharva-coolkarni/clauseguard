RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at New Delhi, Delhi on 22 November 2026 by and between Lakshmi Gupta (the "Landlord") of the one part and Manoj Joshi (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 789, Silver Oak Apartments, Dwarka, New Delhi (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 December 2026 to 31 October 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 39,000 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Landlord may deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 78,000 any amount that he deems fit towards repairs, cleaning, painting or replacement of fittings, and shall not be required to produce any bills or receipts for such deductions.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers at any time during the tenancy, and the Tenant shall not object to such visits.

8. The Tenant shall hand over the Premises in brand-new condition, failing which the Landlord shall be entitled to recover the cost of restoring the Premises to such condition from the Tenant.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Delhi.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
