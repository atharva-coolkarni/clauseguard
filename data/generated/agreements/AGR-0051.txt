RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Mumbai, Maharashtra on 21 March 2026 by and between Vikram Nair (the "Landlord") of the one part and Priya Joshi (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 3 BHK flat No. D-1001 in Sunrise Apartments, Andheri West, Mumbai - 400077, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 April 2026 and ending on 28 February 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 12,000, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. A refundable security deposit of Rs. 24,000 (two months' rent) is payable on signing. Within 15 days of the Tenant vacating, the Landlord shall return the deposit together with an itemised statement of any deductions, supported by bills or receipts.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. The Tenant shall return the Premises in a reasonably clean condition, similar to its condition at the start of the tenancy, normal wear and tear excepted. The Tenant shall not be required to pay for professional deep cleaning or repainting.

8. Except in an emergency, the Landlord shall give at least 24 hours' notice before entering the Premises. In an emergency such as fire, gas leakage or major water leakage, the Landlord may enter without notice and shall inform the Tenant immediately.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Mumbai.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
