RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Bengaluru on 28 March 2026 between Vikram Banerjee, residing at 67, Temple Street, Jayanagar, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Ravi Nair, presently residing at 226, Main Road, Kaloor, Kochi (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 1193 in Orchid Court, HSR Layout, Bengaluru - 560071, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 April 2026 to 28 February 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 33,000, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. Out of the security deposit of Rs. 66,000, an amount equal to one month's rent shall be non-refundable and shall be retained by the Landlord towards maintenance charges, irrespective of the condition of the Premises at the time of vacating.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. The Tenant shall return the Premises in a reasonably clean condition, similar to its condition at the start of the tenancy, normal wear and tear excepted. The Tenant shall not be required to pay for professional deep cleaning or repainting.

8. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Landlord from entering the Premises without notice where it is necessary to prevent immediate danger to life or serious damage to the building, provided the Tenant is informed at once.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Bengaluru.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
