RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Noida on 18 April 2026 between Swati Singh, residing at 235, Church Street, Sector 62, Noida (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Fatima Fernandes, presently residing at 135, MG Road, Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 1220, Lakeview Towers, Sector 18, Noida (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of twelve months commencing on 1 May 2026 and ending on 30 April 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 54,000 on or before the 5th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Landlord may deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 1,08,000 any amount that he deems fit towards repairs, cleaning, painting or replacement of fittings, and shall not be required to produce any bills or receipts for such deductions.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

8. Before vacating the Premises, the Tenant shall, at the Tenant's own cost, get the entire Premises professionally deep cleaned and repainted through a vendor approved by the Landlord, irrespective of the condition of the Premises, failing which the cost shall be recovered from the security deposit.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Noida.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
