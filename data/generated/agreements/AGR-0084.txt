RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Mumbai on 23 July 2026 between Kavya Menon, residing at 106, MG Road, Powai, Mumbai (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Meera Khan, presently residing at 36, Station Road, Saket, New Delhi (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 1495 in Green Valley Residency, Andheri West, Mumbai - 400082, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 August 2026 and ending on 30 June 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 38,000 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. A refundable security deposit of Rs. 76,000 (two months' rent) is payable on signing. Within 7 days of the Tenant vacating, the Landlord shall return the deposit together with an itemised statement of any deductions, supported by bills or receipts.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. A fixed amount of Rs. 25,000 towards painting and deep cleaning shall be deducted from the security deposit when the Tenant vacates, whether or not any painting or cleaning is required.

8. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Landlord from entering the Premises without notice where it is necessary to prevent immediate danger to life or serious damage to the building, provided the Tenant is informed at once.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Maharashtra.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
