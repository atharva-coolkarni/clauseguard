RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Kochi on 21 February 2026 between Nikhil Joshi, residing at 98, MG Road, Kaloor, Kochi (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Venkat Patel, presently residing at 57, Station Road, Anna Nagar, Chennai (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. D-1302, Orchid Court, Kakkanad, Kochi (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 March 2026 and ending on 31 January 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 34,000, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 1,70,000, equal to five months' rent, before taking possession of the Premises. The deposit shall be refunded without interest when the Tenant vacates.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Tenant shall return the Premises in a reasonably clean condition, similar to its condition at the start of the tenancy, normal wear and tear excepted. The Tenant shall not be required to pay for professional deep cleaning or repainting.

8. The Landlord or his authorised agents shall have the right to enter the Premises at any time, with or without notice, for the purpose of inspection.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Kochi.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
