RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Pune, Maharashtra on 25 November 2026 by and between Pooja Kulkarni (the "Landlord") of the one part and Harpreet Fernandes (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. D-1103 in Ganga Residency, Hinjewadi, Pune - 411072, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 December 2026 and ending on 31 October 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 56,500 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. No deduction shall be made from the deposit of Rs. 1,13,000 except for rent arrears, pending utility or society maintenance bills, and the reasonable repair cost of damage caused by the Tenant, excluding ordinary wear and tear. Dated photographs of the Premises shall be taken by both parties when the Tenant moves in and when the Tenant moves out.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. For inspection, repairs or replacements, the Landlord shall enter the Premises only after giving the Tenant written notice at least 24 hours in advance, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

8. When moving out, the Tenant shall clear out all personal belongings and waste, sweep the Premises clean and hand over all keys to the Landlord.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Pune.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
