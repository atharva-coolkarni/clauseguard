RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Kolkata on 24 June 2026 between Harpreet Das, residing at 138, Temple Street, Behala, Kolkata (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Neha Chatterjee, presently residing at 151, Park Avenue, Navrangpura, Ahmedabad (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. C-203, Lakeview Towers, Garia, Kolkata (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of twelve months commencing on 1 July 2026 and ending on 30 June 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 13,000 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Tenant has paid the Landlord an interest-free, refundable security deposit of Rs. 26,000, equal to two months' rent. The Landlord shall refund the deposit on the date the Tenant hands over vacant possession of the Premises, after deducting only rent or utility charges that remain unpaid on that date.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. On vacating, the Tenant shall remove all personal belongings and rubbish and leave the Premises broom-clean, with all keys returned to the Landlord.

8. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Kolkata.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
