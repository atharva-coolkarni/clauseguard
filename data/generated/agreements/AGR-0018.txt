RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Noida, Uttar Pradesh on 17 September 2026 by and between Kavya Das (the "Landlord") of the one part and Ayesha Shetty (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. C-1101 in Shanti Enclave, Sector 50, Noida - 201081, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 October 2026 to 31 August 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 56,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 1,13,000 may be adjusted only against (a) unpaid rent, (b) unpaid electricity, water or maintenance bills, and (c) the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Both parties shall record the condition of the Premises with dated photographs at move-in and move-out.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. Except in an emergency, the Landlord shall give at least 24 hours' notice before entering the Premises. In an emergency such as fire, gas leakage or major water leakage, the Landlord may enter without notice and shall inform the Tenant immediately.

8. Normal wear and tear arising from ordinary residential use, including fading or minor marks on painted walls, shall not be treated as damage and shall not be charged to the Tenant at the end of the tenancy.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Noida.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
