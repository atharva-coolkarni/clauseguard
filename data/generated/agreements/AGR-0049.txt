RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Chennai, Tamil Nadu on 25 November 2026 by and between Divya Patel (the "Landlord") of the one part and Fatima Patel (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 1177, Shanti Enclave, Thoraipakkam, Chennai (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 December 2026 and ending on 31 October 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 34,500, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. If the Tenant vacates the Premises before the expiry of the term for any reason whatsoever, including after giving notice, the entire security deposit of Rs. 69,000 shall stand forfeited to the Landlord.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers at any time during the tenancy, and the Tenant shall not object to such visits.

8. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, whitewashing of walls and painting of doors and windows. The Tenant shall attend to minor day-to-day repairs such as replacement of switches and sockets, repair of taps and kitchen fixtures, and cleaning of drains.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Chennai.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
