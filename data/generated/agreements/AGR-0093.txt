RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Bengaluru, Karnataka on 27 August 2026 by and between Shreya Gupta (the "Landlord") of the one part and Rohan Joshi (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. B-1103 in Ganga Residency, Indiranagar, Bengaluru - 560026, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 September 2026 to 31 July 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 8,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 8,500 shall be refunded to the Tenant at the sole discretion of the Landlord after the Tenant vacates the Premises, subject to such deductions as the Landlord may consider appropriate.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. Except in an emergency, the Landlord shall give at least 24 hours' notice before entering the Premises. In an emergency such as fire, gas leakage or major water leakage, the Landlord may enter without notice and shall inform the Tenant immediately.

8. All repairs of whatever nature, including structural repairs, roof leakage, seepage and plumbing work inside the walls, shall be carried out by the Tenant at the Tenant's own cost during the tenancy.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Karnataka.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
