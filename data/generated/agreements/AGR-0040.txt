RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Chennai, Tamil Nadu on 18 January 2026 by and between Venkat Das (the "Landlord") of the one part and Pooja Pillai (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 1468, Ganga Residency, Anna Nagar, Chennai (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 February 2026 and ending on 31 December 2026, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 12,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. A refundable security deposit of Rs. 25,000 (two months' rent) is payable on signing. Within 7 days of the Tenant vacating, the Landlord shall return the deposit together with an itemised statement of any deductions, supported by bills or receipts.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Tenant shall return the Premises in a reasonably clean condition, similar to its condition at the start of the tenancy, normal wear and tear excepted. The Tenant shall not be required to pay for professional deep cleaning or repainting.

8. The Landlord or his authorised agents shall have the right to enter the Premises at any time, with or without notice, for the purpose of inspection.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Tamil Nadu.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
