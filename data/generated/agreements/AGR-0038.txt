RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Kochi, Kerala on 17 May 2026 by and between Fatima Khan (the "Landlord") of the one part and Priya Banerjee (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 1001, Green Valley Residency, Vyttila, Kochi (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 June 2026 to 30 April 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 49,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. No deduction shall be made from the deposit of Rs. 99,000 except for rent arrears, pending utility or society maintenance bills, and the reasonable repair cost of damage caused by the Tenant, excluding ordinary wear and tear. Dated photographs of the Premises shall be taken by both parties when the Tenant moves in and when the Tenant moves out.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. In the final month of the tenancy, the Landlord may bring prospective tenants to view the Premises, giving at least 24 hours' notice and at times agreed with the Tenant.

8. When moving out, the Tenant shall clear out all personal belongings and waste, sweep the Premises clean and hand over all keys to the Landlord.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Kerala.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
