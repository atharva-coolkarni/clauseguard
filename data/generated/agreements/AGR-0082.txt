RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Hyderabad, Telangana on 24 September 2026 by and between Lakshmi Sharma (the "Landlord") of the one part and Rohan Reddy (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. C-1104 in Shanti Enclave, Kondapur, Hyderabad - 500087, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 October 2026 to 31 August 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 22,000, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. A refundable security deposit of Rs. 44,000 (two months' rent) is payable on signing. Within 15 days of the Tenant vacating, the Landlord shall return the deposit together with an itemised statement of any deductions, supported by bills or receipts.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. The Landlord may inspect the Premises at any time after informing the Tenant by phone or message at least 4 hour(s) in advance.

8. The Tenant shall return the Premises in a reasonably clean condition, similar to its condition at the start of the tenancy, normal wear and tear excepted. The Tenant shall not be required to pay for professional deep cleaning or repainting.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Hyderabad.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
