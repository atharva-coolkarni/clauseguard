RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Chennai on 21 August 2026 between Fatima Rao, residing at 62, Temple Street, T. Nagar, Chennai (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Sanjay Gupta, presently residing at 13, Church Street, Baner, Pune (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 254 in Ganga Residency, Velachery, Chennai - 600065, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 September 2026 to 31 July 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 8,500, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Landlord shall have the right to recover from the security deposit of Rs. 17,000 such sums as he considers proper for repairs, cleaning, painting or replacement of any items, without being obliged to show any bill or receipt to the Tenant.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. When moving out, the Tenant shall clear out all personal belongings and waste, sweep the Premises clean and hand over all keys to the Landlord.

8. For inspection, repairs or replacements, the Landlord shall enter the Premises only after giving the Tenant written notice at least 24 hours in advance, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Chennai.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
