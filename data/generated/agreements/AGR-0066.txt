RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Noida on 23 December 2025 between Divya Chatterjee, residing at 27, Temple Street, Sector 137, Noida (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Sanjay Mehta, presently residing at 226, Temple Street, HSR Layout, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. D-202, Lakeview Towers, Sector 62, Noida (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 January 2026 and ending on 30 November 2026, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 60,000, payable in advance on or before the 5th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. Out of the security deposit of Rs. 1,20,000, an amount equal to one month's rent shall be non-refundable and shall be retained by the Landlord towards maintenance charges, irrespective of the condition of the Premises at the time of vacating.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, whitewashing of walls and painting of doors and windows. The Tenant shall attend to minor day-to-day repairs such as replacement of switches and sockets, repair of taps and kitchen fixtures, and cleaning of drains.

8. Except in an emergency, the Landlord shall give at least 24 hours' notice before entering the Premises. In an emergency such as fire, gas leakage or major water leakage, the Landlord may enter without notice and shall inform the Tenant immediately.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Uttar Pradesh.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
