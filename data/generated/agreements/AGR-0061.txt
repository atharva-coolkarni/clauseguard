RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at New Delhi, Delhi on 27 October 2026 by and between Joseph Joshi (the "Landlord") of the one part and Imran Singh (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. B-804 in Sunrise Apartments, Rohini, New Delhi - 110051, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 November 2026 to 30 September 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 19,500, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. On signing this Agreement, the Tenant has deposited Rs. 39,000 (two months' rent) with the Landlord as security. This deposit carries no interest and shall be returned to the Tenant when vacant possession is handed back, less only any rent or utility bills still unpaid on that day.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. In the final month of the tenancy, the Landlord may bring prospective tenants to view the Premises, giving at least 24 hours' notice and at times agreed with the Tenant.

8. At the end of the tenancy the Tenant shall leave the Premises reasonably clean, in much the same state as when the Tenant moved in, allowing for normal wear and tear. No charge for professional deep cleaning or repainting shall be made to the Tenant.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Delhi.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
