RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Bengaluru on 20 December 2025 between Meera Singh, residing at 120, Park Avenue, Indiranagar, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Neha Iyer, presently residing at 197, Temple Street, Kondapur, Hyderabad (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. B-304 in Lakeview Towers, Koramangala, Bengaluru - 560080, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 January 2026 and ending on 30 November 2026, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 20,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. Any rent or utility charges remaining unpaid when the Tenant vacates, and the cost of making good any damage caused by the Tenant, may be recovered by the Landlord out of the security deposit of Rs. 41,000, provided that the Landlord first shows the Tenant the relevant bills.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. When moving out, the Tenant shall clear out all personal belongings and waste, sweep the Premises clean and hand over all keys to the Landlord.

8. A phone call or text message to the Tenant at least 4 hour(s) beforehand shall be sufficient notice for the Landlord to inspect the Premises at any time.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Karnataka.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
