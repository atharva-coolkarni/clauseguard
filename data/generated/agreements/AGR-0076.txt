RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Pune on 23 February 2026 between Rohan Das, residing at 240, Main Road, Baner, Pune (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Ravi Das, presently residing at 85, Park Avenue, Adyar, Chennai (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. B-1003 in Sunrise Apartments, Aundh, Pune - 411051, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 March 2026 to 31 January 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 52,500 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. Out of the security deposit of Rs. 1,05,000, an amount equal to one month's rent shall be non-refundable and shall be retained by the Landlord towards maintenance charges, irrespective of the condition of the Premises at the time of vacating.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

8. The Tenant shall hand over the Premises in brand-new condition, failing which the Landlord shall be entitled to recover the cost of restoring the Premises to such condition from the Tenant.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Maharashtra.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
