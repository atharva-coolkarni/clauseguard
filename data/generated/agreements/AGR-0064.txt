RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Mumbai on 21 June 2026 between Manoj Chatterjee, residing at 37, Church Street, Goregaon East, Mumbai (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Nikhil Nair, presently residing at 161, Main Road, Viman Nagar, Pune (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 3 BHK flat No. 607 in Lakeview Towers, Powai, Mumbai - 400090, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 July 2026 to 31 May 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 25,500, payable in advance on or before the 5th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Landlord shall be entitled to deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 51,000 any rent or utility dues outstanding on the date of vacating, and the cost of repairing any damage caused by the Tenant, as shown by bills which the Landlord shall share with the Tenant before making the deduction.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. The Tenant shall bear the cost of repairing any damage to the Premises or its fittings caused by the Tenant, members of the Tenant's family or the Tenant's guests, other than normal wear and tear.

8. During the last month of the tenancy, the Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants after giving 24 hours' notice, at times mutually convenient to both parties.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Mumbai.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
