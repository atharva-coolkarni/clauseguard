RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Kolkata on 17 May 2026 between Manoj Reddy, residing at 196, Temple Street, Garia, Kolkata (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Lakshmi Shetty, presently residing at 58, Main Road, Malad West, Mumbai (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. 818 in Lakeview Towers, Garia, Kolkata - 700028, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 June 2026 and ending on 30 April 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 44,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 89,000 shall be refunded to the Tenant at the sole discretion of the Landlord after the Tenant vacates the Premises, subject to such deductions as the Landlord may consider appropriate.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. A fixed amount of Rs. 25,000 towards painting and deep cleaning shall be deducted from the security deposit when the Tenant vacates, whether or not any painting or cleaning is required.

8. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Kolkata.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
