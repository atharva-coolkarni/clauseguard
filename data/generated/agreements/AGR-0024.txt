RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Mumbai, Maharashtra on 19 September 2026 by and between Arjun Gupta (the "Landlord") of the one part and Suresh Sharma (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 1372 in Orchid Court, Powai, Mumbai - 400078, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is twelve months, from 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 26,000 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. If the Tenant vacates the Premises before the expiry of the term for any reason whatsoever, including after giving notice, the entire security deposit of Rs. 52,000 shall stand forfeited to the Landlord.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. The Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers at any time during the tenancy, and the Tenant shall not object to such visits.

8. The Tenant shall inform the Landlord in writing of any repair that is the Landlord's responsibility. If the Landlord does not carry out the repair within 15 days of such notice, the Tenant may get it done and adjust the reasonable cost, supported by receipts, against the next month's rent.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Mumbai.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
