RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Chennai, Tamil Nadu on 21 August 2026 by and between Meera Fernandes (the "Landlord") of the one part and Lakshmi Iyer (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 865 in Green Valley Residency, Thoraipakkam, Chennai - 600036, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is twelve months, from 1 September 2026 to 31 August 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 56,500 on or before the 5th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Tenant shall keep with the Landlord a refundable deposit of Rs. 1,13,000, being two months' rent. The Landlord shall repay it within 15 days after the Tenant moves out, and shall give the Tenant a written list of any amounts deducted along with the supporting bills.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. In the final month of the tenancy, the Landlord may bring prospective tenants to view the Premises, giving at least 24 hours' notice and at times agreed with the Tenant.

8. At the end of the tenancy the Tenant shall leave the Premises reasonably clean, in much the same state as when the Tenant moved in, allowing for normal wear and tear. No charge for professional deep cleaning or repainting shall be made to the Tenant.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Tamil Nadu.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
