RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Pune on 28 January 2026 between Vikram Sharma, residing at 13, Park Avenue, Kothrud, Pune (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Ayesha Chatterjee, presently residing at 107, Main Road, Anna Nagar, Chennai (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. A-1001, Maple Heights, Kothrud, Pune (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 February 2026 to 31 December 2026.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 53,500, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. No deduction shall be made from the deposit of Rs. 1,07,000 except for rent arrears, pending utility or society maintenance bills, and the reasonable repair cost of damage caused by the Tenant, excluding ordinary wear and tear. Dated photographs of the Premises shall be taken by both parties when the Tenant moves in and when the Tenant moves out.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. At the end of the tenancy the Tenant shall leave the Premises reasonably clean, in much the same state as when the Tenant moved in, allowing for normal wear and tear. No charge for professional deep cleaning or repainting shall be made to the Tenant.

8. The Landlord and any person authorised by him may enter and inspect the Premises at any hour, whether or not the Tenant has been given notice.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Pune.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
