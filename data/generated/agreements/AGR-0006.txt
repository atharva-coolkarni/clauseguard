RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Kochi, Kerala on 23 January 2026 by and between Sneha Reddy (the "Landlord") of the one part and Neha Fernandes (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. B-1404, Ganga Residency, Kaloor, Kochi (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 February 2026 and ending on 31 December 2026, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 18,500, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 1,85,000, equal to ten months' rent, before taking possession of the Premises. The deposit shall be refunded without interest when the Tenant vacates.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. Before vacating the Premises, the Tenant shall, at the Tenant's own cost, get the entire Premises professionally deep cleaned and repainted through a vendor approved by the Landlord, irrespective of the condition of the Premises, failing which the cost shall be recovered from the security deposit.

8. Except in an emergency, the Landlord shall give at least 24 hours' notice before entering the Premises. In an emergency such as fire, gas leakage or major water leakage, the Landlord may enter without notice and shall inform the Tenant immediately.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Kochi.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
