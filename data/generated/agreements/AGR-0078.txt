RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Noida, Uttar Pradesh on 22 December 2025 by and between Joseph Pillai (the "Landlord") of the one part and Karthik Reddy (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. B-201, Ganga Residency, Sector 137, Noida (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is twelve months, from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 13,500, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Landlord may deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 27,000 any amount that he deems fit towards repairs, cleaning, painting or replacement of fittings, and shall not be required to produce any bills or receipts for such deductions.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. Normal wear and tear arising from ordinary residential use, including fading or minor marks on painted walls, shall not be treated as damage and shall not be charged to the Tenant at the end of the tenancy.

8. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Noida.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
