RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Ahmedabad, Gujarat on 18 August 2026 by and between Suresh Menon (the "Landlord") of the one part and Swati Das (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. A-204, Sunrise Apartments, Maninagar, Ahmedabad (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 September 2026 to 31 July 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 44,000, payable in advance on or before the 5th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. One month's rent out of the security deposit of Rs. 88,000 shall not be returned to the Tenant in any circumstances and shall be kept by the Landlord as a maintenance fee, whatever the state of the Premises when the Tenant leaves.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Landlord shall not enter the Premises without at least 24 hours' notice to the Tenant, except in an emergency such as a fire, a gas leak or a serious water leak, in which case the Landlord may enter at once but must inform the Tenant immediately.

8. Structural repairs, whitewashing of the walls and painting of doors and windows are the responsibility of the Landlord. Minor routine repairs, such as fixing switches, sockets, taps and kitchen fittings and keeping the drains clean, shall be handled by the Tenant.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Ahmedabad.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
