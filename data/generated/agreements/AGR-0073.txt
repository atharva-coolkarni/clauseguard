RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Pune, Maharashtra on 27 March 2026 by and between Sneha Sharma (the "Landlord") of the one part and Priya Singh (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 505, Maple Heights, Kothrud, Pune (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is twelve months, from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 31,500, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. Out of the security deposit of Rs. 63,000, an amount equal to one month's rent shall be non-refundable and shall be retained by the Landlord towards maintenance charges, irrespective of the condition of the Premises at the time of vacating.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. Normal wear and tear arising from ordinary residential use, including fading or minor marks on painted walls, shall not be treated as damage and shall not be charged to the Tenant at the end of the tenancy.

8. The Landlord shall retain a duplicate key to the Premises and may enter the Premises in the absence of the Tenant whenever he considers it necessary.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Maharashtra.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
