RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Bengaluru, Karnataka on 21 June 2026 by and between Aditya Reddy (the "Landlord") of the one part and Lakshmi Rao (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 1349 in Lakeview Towers, Jayanagar, Bengaluru - 560053, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 July 2026 and ending on 31 May 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 23,500 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 47,000 may be adjusted only against (a) unpaid rent, (b) unpaid electricity, water or maintenance bills, and (c) the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Both parties shall record the condition of the Premises with dated photographs at move-in and move-out.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Tenant shall inform the Landlord in writing of any repair that is the Landlord's responsibility. If the Landlord does not carry out the repair within 7 days of such notice, the Tenant may get it done and adjust the reasonable cost, supported by receipts, against the next month's rent.

8. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Landlord from entering the Premises without notice where it is necessary to prevent immediate danger to life or serious damage to the building, provided the Tenant is informed at once.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Karnataka.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
