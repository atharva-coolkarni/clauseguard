RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Hyderabad, Telangana on 28 January 2026 by and between Harpreet Menon (the "Landlord") of the one part and Arjun Joshi (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 3 BHK flat No. 1362 in Lakeview Towers, Kukatpally, Hyderabad - 500091, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is twelve months, from 1 February 2026 to 31 January 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 31,000 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. No deduction shall be made from the deposit of Rs. 62,000 except for rent arrears, pending utility or society maintenance bills, and the reasonable repair cost of damage caused by the Tenant, excluding ordinary wear and tear. Dated photographs of the Premises shall be taken by both parties when the Tenant moves in and when the Tenant moves out.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. A phone call or text message to the Tenant at least 2 hour(s) beforehand shall be sufficient notice for the Landlord to inspect the Premises at any time.

8. Whatever the condition of the Premises, the Tenant shall pay for a professional deep cleaning and a full repainting by a vendor chosen by the Landlord before moving out; if the Tenant does not, the Landlord may take the cost out of the security deposit.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Hyderabad.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
