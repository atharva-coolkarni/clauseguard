RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Hyderabad, Telangana on 23 September 2026 by and between Suresh Singh (the "Landlord") of the one part and Harpreet Chatterjee (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. B-601 in Ganga Residency, Gachibowli, Hyderabad - 500051, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 October 2026 to 31 August 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 36,500 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Landlord shall be entitled to deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 73,000 any rent or utility dues outstanding on the date of vacating, and the cost of repairing any damage caused by the Tenant, as shown by bills which the Landlord shall share with the Tenant before making the deduction.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. The Tenant shall bear the cost of periodic servicing and repair of all appliances provided by the Landlord, including air conditioners, geysers, water purifiers and the refrigerator.

8. The Landlord may inspect the Premises at any time after informing the Tenant by phone or message at least 4 hour(s) in advance.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Hyderabad.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
