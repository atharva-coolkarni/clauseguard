RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Hyderabad, Telangana on 23 April 2026 by and between Kavya Verma (the "Landlord") of the one part and Lakshmi Sharma (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. B-904 in Ganga Residency, Madhapur, Hyderabad - 500075, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 May 2026 and ending on 31 March 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 54,000 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 1,08,000 may be adjusted only against (a) unpaid rent, (b) unpaid electricity, water or maintenance bills, and (c) the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Both parties shall record the condition of the Premises with dated photographs at move-in and move-out.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. The Tenant shall bear the cost of repairing any damage to the Premises or its fittings caused by the Tenant, members of the Tenant's family or the Tenant's guests, other than normal wear and tear.

8. During the last month of the tenancy, the Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants after giving 24 hours' notice, at times mutually convenient to both parties.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Telangana.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
