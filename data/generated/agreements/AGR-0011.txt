RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Chennai, Tamil Nadu on 22 April 2026 by and between Harpreet Pillai (the "Landlord") of the one part and Anil Kulkarni (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. B-1302 in Maple Heights, Velachery, Chennai - 600087, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 May 2026 to 31 March 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 13,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 40,500, equal to three months' rent, before taking possession of the Premises. The deposit shall be refunded without interest when the Tenant vacates.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. The Tenant shall bear the cost of periodic servicing and repair of all appliances provided by the Landlord, including air conditioners, geysers, water purifiers and the refrigerator.

8. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Tamil Nadu.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
