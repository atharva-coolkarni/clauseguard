RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Ahmedabad, Gujarat on 27 September 2026 by and between Aditya Kulkarni (the "Landlord") of the one part and Meera Das (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 3 BHK flat No. 1456 in Sunrise Apartments, Satellite, Ahmedabad - 380021, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 October 2026 and ending on 31 August 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 11,000 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. If the Tenant vacates the Premises before the expiry of the term for any reason whatsoever, including after giving notice, the entire security deposit of Rs. 22,000 shall stand forfeited to the Landlord.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. The Landlord shall retain a duplicate key to the Premises and may enter the Premises in the absence of the Tenant whenever he considers it necessary.

8. The Tenant shall inform the Landlord in writing of any repair that is the Landlord's responsibility. If the Landlord does not carry out the repair within 15 days of such notice, the Tenant may get it done and adjust the reasonable cost, supported by receipts, against the next month's rent.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Ahmedabad.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
