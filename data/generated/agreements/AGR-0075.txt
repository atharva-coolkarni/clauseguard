RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Chennai on 29 July 2026 between Swati Pillai, residing at 207, Station Road, T. Nagar, Chennai (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Joseph Das, presently residing at 184, Station Road, Hinjewadi, Pune (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 102, Shanti Enclave, Adyar, Chennai (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 August 2026 to 30 June 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 13,500 on or before the 5th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Landlord may deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 27,000 any amount that he deems fit towards repairs, cleaning, painting or replacement of fittings, and shall not be required to produce any bills or receipts for such deductions.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. A fixed amount of Rs. 20,000 towards painting and deep cleaning shall be deducted from the security deposit when the Tenant vacates, whether or not any painting or cleaning is required.

8. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Landlord from entering the Premises without notice where it is necessary to prevent immediate danger to life or serious damage to the building, provided the Tenant is informed at once.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Tamil Nadu.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
