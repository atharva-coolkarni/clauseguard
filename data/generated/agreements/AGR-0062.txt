RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Hyderabad, Telangana on 19 June 2026 by and between Ananya Rao (the "Landlord") of the one part and Pooja Patel (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. D-304, Silver Oak Apartments, Kondapur, Hyderabad (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of twelve months commencing on 1 July 2026 and ending on 30 June 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 46,000, payable in advance on or before the 5th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Landlord shall be entitled to deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 92,000 any rent or utility dues outstanding on the date of vacating, and the cost of repairing any damage caused by the Tenant, as shown by bills which the Landlord shall share with the Tenant before making the deduction.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Tenant shall bear the cost of repairing any damage to the Premises or its fittings caused by the Tenant, members of the Tenant's family or the Tenant's guests, other than normal wear and tear.

8. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Telangana.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
