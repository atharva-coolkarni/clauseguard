RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Ahmedabad, Gujarat on 18 January 2026 by and between Meera Reddy (the "Landlord") of the one part and Divya Pillai (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. C-704 in Shanti Enclave, Satellite, Ahmedabad - 380006, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 February 2026 to 31 December 2026.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 31,500 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. Any rent or utility charges remaining unpaid when the Tenant vacates, and the cost of making good any damage caused by the Tenant, may be recovered by the Landlord out of the security deposit of Rs. 63,000, provided that the Landlord first shows the Tenant the relevant bills.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. Where a repair falls within the Landlord's responsibility, the Tenant shall notify the Landlord in writing. Should the Landlord fail to complete the repair within 15 days of the notice, the Tenant may arrange the repair and set off the reasonable cost, backed by receipts, against the following month's rent.

8. For inspection, repairs or replacements, the Landlord shall enter the Premises only after giving the Tenant written notice at least 24 hours in advance, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Ahmedabad.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
