RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Pune, Maharashtra on 26 November 2026 by and between Aditya Rao (the "Landlord") of the one part and Nikhil Mehta (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 657, Green Valley Residency, Hinjewadi, Pune (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 December 2026 to 31 October 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 31,500, payable in advance on or before the 5th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. A refundable security deposit of Rs. 63,000 (two months' rent) is payable on signing. Within 15 days of the Tenant vacating, the Landlord shall return the deposit together with an itemised statement of any deductions, supported by bills or receipts.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, whitewashing of walls and painting of doors and windows. The Tenant shall attend to minor day-to-day repairs such as replacement of switches and sockets, repair of taps and kitchen fixtures, and cleaning of drains.

8. The Landlord may inspect the Premises at any time after informing the Tenant by phone or message at least 1 hour(s) in advance.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Pune.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
