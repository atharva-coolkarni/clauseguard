RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Hyderabad on 26 September 2026 between Ravi Fernandes, residing at 12, MG Road, Gachibowli, Hyderabad (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Karthik Khan, presently residing at 198, Station Road, Saket, New Delhi (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 290, Maple Heights, Gachibowli, Hyderabad (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 October 2026 and ending on 31 August 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 53,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 1,07,000 may be adjusted only against (a) unpaid rent, (b) unpaid electricity, water or maintenance bills, and (c) the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Both parties shall record the condition of the Premises with dated photographs at move-in and move-out.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. All repairs of whatever nature, including structural repairs, roof leakage, seepage and plumbing work inside the walls, shall be carried out by the Tenant at the Tenant's own cost during the tenancy.

8. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Hyderabad.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
