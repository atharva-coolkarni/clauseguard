RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Kolkata, West Bengal on 16 June 2026 by and between Deepa Verma (the "Landlord") of the one part and Divya Fernandes (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. D-501, Shanti Enclave, Behala, Kolkata (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of twelve months commencing on 1 July 2026 and ending on 30 June 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 42,000, payable in advance on or before the 5th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 1,26,000, equal to three months' rent, before taking possession of the Premises. The deposit shall be refunded without interest when the Tenant vacates.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. During the last month of the tenancy, the Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants after giving 24 hours' notice, at times mutually convenient to both parties.

8. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, whitewashing of walls and painting of doors and windows. The Tenant shall attend to minor day-to-day repairs such as replacement of switches and sockets, repair of taps and kitchen fixtures, and cleaning of drains.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in West Bengal.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
