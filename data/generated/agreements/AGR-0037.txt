RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at New Delhi, Delhi on 26 August 2026 by and between Divya Verma (the "Landlord") of the one part and Kavya Kulkarni (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 190, Sunrise Apartments, Mayur Vihar, New Delhi (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 September 2026 and ending on 31 July 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 12,000, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. A refundable security deposit of Rs. 24,000 (two months' rent) is payable on signing. Within 15 days of the Tenant vacating, the Landlord shall return the deposit together with an itemised statement of any deductions, supported by bills or receipts.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. The Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers at any time during the tenancy, and the Tenant shall not object to such visits.

8. A fixed amount of Rs. 8,000 towards painting and deep cleaning shall be deducted from the security deposit when the Tenant vacates, whether or not any painting or cleaning is required.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Delhi.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
