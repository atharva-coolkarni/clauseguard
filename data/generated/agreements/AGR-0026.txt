RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Pune, Maharashtra on 28 September 2026 by and between Nikhil Joshi (the "Landlord") of the one part and Neha Nair (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 584, Orchid Court, Kothrud, Pune (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of twelve months commencing on 1 October 2026 and ending on 30 September 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 25,500 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 25,500 shall be refunded to the Tenant at the sole discretion of the Landlord after the Tenant vacates the Premises, subject to such deductions as the Landlord may consider appropriate.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. Before vacating the Premises, the Tenant shall, at the Tenant's own cost, get the entire Premises professionally deep cleaned and repainted through a vendor approved by the Landlord, irrespective of the condition of the Premises, failing which the cost shall be recovered from the security deposit.

8. The Landlord shall retain a duplicate key to the Premises and may enter the Premises in the absence of the Tenant whenever he considers it necessary.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Pune.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
