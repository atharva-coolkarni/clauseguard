RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Hyderabad, Telangana on 21 July 2026 by and between Lakshmi Das (the "Landlord") of the one part and Venkat Iyer (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 1218, Lakeview Towers, Madhapur, Hyderabad (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is twelve months, from 1 August 2026 to 31 July 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 33,000 on or before the 5th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. No deduction shall be made from the deposit of Rs. 66,000 except for rent arrears, pending utility or society maintenance bills, and the reasonable repair cost of damage caused by the Tenant, excluding ordinary wear and tear. Dated photographs of the Premises shall be taken by both parties when the Tenant moves in and when the Tenant moves out.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. The Landlord shall keep a spare key to the Premises and shall be free to enter the Premises when the Tenant is not present, whenever he thinks fit.

8. Where a repair falls within the Landlord's responsibility, the Tenant shall notify the Landlord in writing. Should the Landlord fail to complete the repair within 7 days of the notice, the Tenant may arrange the repair and set off the reasonable cost, backed by receipts, against the following month's rent.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Hyderabad.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
