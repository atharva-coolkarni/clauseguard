RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Hyderabad, Telangana on 26 November 2026 by and between Lakshmi Iyer (the "Landlord") of the one part and Suresh Nair (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 497, Shanti Enclave, Gachibowli, Hyderabad (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of twelve months commencing on 1 December 2026 and ending on 30 November 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 29,000, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. Out of the security deposit of Rs. 58,000, an amount equal to one month's rent shall be non-refundable and shall be retained by the Landlord towards maintenance charges, irrespective of the condition of the Premises at the time of vacating.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Tenant shall bear the cost of periodic servicing and repair of all appliances provided by the Landlord, including air conditioners, geysers, water purifiers and the refrigerator.

8. The Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers at any time during the tenancy, and the Tenant shall not object to such visits.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Telangana.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
