RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Hyderabad on 22 August 2026 between Ananya Shetty, residing at 233, MG Road, Begumpet, Hyderabad (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Rahul Khan, presently residing at 67, Church Street, Thoraipakkam, Chennai (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 1292 in Green Valley Residency, Madhapur, Hyderabad - 500094, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 September 2026 and ending on 31 July 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 24,500, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Tenant has paid the Landlord an interest-free, refundable security deposit of Rs. 49,000, equal to two months' rent. The Landlord shall refund the deposit on the date the Tenant hands over vacant possession of the Premises, after deducting only rent or utility charges that remain unpaid on that date.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. Except in an emergency, the Landlord shall give at least 24 hours' notice before entering the Premises. In an emergency such as fire, gas leakage or major water leakage, the Landlord may enter without notice and shall inform the Tenant immediately.

8. Normal wear and tear arising from ordinary residential use, including fading or minor marks on painted walls, shall not be treated as damage and shall not be charged to the Tenant at the end of the tenancy.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Hyderabad.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
