RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Kochi on 25 February 2026 between Vikram Rao, residing at 214, Church Street, Kaloor, Kochi (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Pooja Joshi, presently residing at 39, MG Road, Malad West, Mumbai (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. C-401 in Ganga Residency, Kaloor, Kochi - 682027, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 March 2026 to 31 January 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 43,000, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. On signing this Agreement, the Tenant has deposited Rs. 43,000 (one month's rent) with the Landlord as security. This deposit carries no interest and shall be returned to the Tenant when vacant possession is handed back, less only any rent or utility bills still unpaid on that day.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. All appliances supplied by the Landlord, such as the air conditioners, geyser, water purifier and refrigerator, shall be serviced and repaired at the Tenant's expense throughout the tenancy.

8. The Landlord and any person authorised by him may enter and inspect the Premises at any hour, whether or not the Tenant has been given notice.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Kochi.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
