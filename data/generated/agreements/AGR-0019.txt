RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Noida, Uttar Pradesh on 19 November 2026 by and between Shreya Das (the "Landlord") of the one part and Imran Nair (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 985, Ganga Residency, Sector 18, Noida (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 December 2026 and ending on 31 October 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 52,500, payable in advance on or before the 5th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. Any rent or utility charges remaining unpaid when the Tenant vacates, and the cost of making good any damage caused by the Tenant, may be recovered by the Landlord out of the security deposit of Rs. 1,05,000, provided that the Landlord first shows the Tenant the relevant bills.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. Any damage to the Premises or its fittings caused by the Tenant, the Tenant's family members or visitors shall be repaired at the Tenant's cost, except ordinary wear and tear.

8. The Landlord may enter the Premises without notice only where this is needed to prevent an immediate risk to life or serious damage to the building, and shall inform the Tenant straight away.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Uttar Pradesh.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
