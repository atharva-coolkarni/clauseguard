RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Bengaluru, Karnataka on 18 October 2026 by and between Rahul Reddy (the "Landlord") of the one part and Neha Verma (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 1415, Sunrise Apartments, Jayanagar, Bengaluru (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 November 2026 to 30 September 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 57,000 on or before the 5th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 1,14,000 shall be refunded to the Tenant at the sole discretion of the Landlord after the Tenant vacates the Premises, subject to such deductions as the Landlord may consider appropriate.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

8. The Tenant shall hand over the Premises in brand-new condition, failing which the Landlord shall be entitled to recover the cost of restoring the Premises to such condition from the Tenant.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Karnataka.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
