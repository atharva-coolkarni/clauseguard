RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Ahmedabad, Gujarat on 17 August 2026 by and between Karthik Khan (the "Landlord") of the one part and Karthik Singh (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. 1144 in Silver Oak Apartments, Vastrapur, Ahmedabad - 380055, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 September 2026 to 31 July 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 55,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Tenant has paid the Landlord an interest-free, refundable security deposit of Rs. 1,11,000, equal to two months' rent. The Landlord shall refund the deposit on the date the Tenant hands over vacant possession of the Premises, after deducting only rent or utility charges that remain unpaid on that date.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. The Landlord shall retain a duplicate key to the Premises and may enter the Premises in the absence of the Tenant whenever he considers it necessary.

8. On vacating, the Tenant shall remove all personal belongings and rubbish and leave the Premises broom-clean, with all keys returned to the Landlord.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Ahmedabad.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
