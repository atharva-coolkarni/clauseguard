RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Noida, Uttar Pradesh on 17 September 2026 by and between Kavya Rao (the "Landlord") of the one part and Ravi Menon (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. A-1204 in Maple Heights, Sector 76, Noida - 201025, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 October 2026 to 31 August 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 32,500 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Landlord may deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 65,000 any amount that he deems fit towards repairs, cleaning, painting or replacement of fittings, and shall not be required to produce any bills or receipts for such deductions.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, whitewashing of walls and painting of doors and windows. The Tenant shall attend to minor day-to-day repairs such as replacement of switches and sockets, repair of taps and kitchen fixtures, and cleaning of drains.

8. The Landlord shall retain a duplicate key to the Premises and may enter the Premises in the absence of the Tenant whenever he considers it necessary.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Uttar Pradesh.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
