RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Ahmedabad on 20 November 2026 between Rohan Sharma, residing at 178, Temple Street, Bopal, Ahmedabad (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Priya Fernandes, presently residing at 34, MG Road, Anna Nagar, Chennai (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. A-1101 in Shanti Enclave, Bopal, Ahmedabad - 380097, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 December 2026 to 31 October 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 8,500, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 17,000 may be adjusted only against (a) unpaid rent, (b) unpaid electricity, water or maintenance bills, and (c) the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Both parties shall record the condition of the Premises with dated photographs at move-in and move-out.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Landlord from entering the Premises without notice where it is necessary to prevent immediate danger to life or serious damage to the building, provided the Tenant is informed at once.

8. A fixed amount of Rs. 12,000 towards painting and deep cleaning shall be deducted from the security deposit when the Tenant vacates, whether or not any painting or cleaning is required.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Ahmedabad.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
