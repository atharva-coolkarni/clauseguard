RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Pune, Maharashtra on 22 November 2026 by and between Rahul Patel (the "Landlord") of the one part and Suresh Shetty (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. D-904 in Maple Heights, Aundh, Pune - 411068, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 December 2026 to 31 October 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 32,000 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Landlord shall be entitled to deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 64,000 any rent or utility dues outstanding on the date of vacating, and the cost of repairing any damage caused by the Tenant, as shown by bills which the Landlord shall share with the Tenant before making the deduction.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. The Landlord or his authorised agents shall have the right to enter the Premises at any time, with or without notice, for the purpose of inspection.

8. The Tenant shall inform the Landlord in writing of any repair that is the Landlord's responsibility. If the Landlord does not carry out the repair within 7 days of such notice, the Tenant may get it done and adjust the reasonable cost, supported by receipts, against the next month's rent.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Pune.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
