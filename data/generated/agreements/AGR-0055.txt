RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Mumbai on 25 January 2026 between Arjun Chatterjee, residing at 96, Main Road, Chembur, Mumbai (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Swati Joshi, presently residing at 145, Station Road, Baner, Pune (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 648, Shanti Enclave, Powai, Mumbai (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 February 2026 to 31 December 2026.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 41,500, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Landlord may deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 83,000 any amount that he deems fit towards repairs, cleaning, painting or replacement of fittings, and shall not be required to produce any bills or receipts for such deductions.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. The Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers at any time during the tenancy, and the Tenant shall not object to such visits.

8. The Tenant shall inform the Landlord in writing of any repair that is the Landlord's responsibility. If the Landlord does not carry out the repair within 7 days of such notice, the Tenant may get it done and adjust the reasonable cost, supported by receipts, against the next month's rent.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Mumbai.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
