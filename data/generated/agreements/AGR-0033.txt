RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Kochi on 16 November 2026 between Karthik Reddy, residing at 100, Main Road, Vyttila, Kochi (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Imran Nair, presently residing at 174, Church Street, Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 846 in Shanti Enclave, Edappally, Kochi - 682006, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 December 2026 to 31 October 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 40,000, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. A refundable security deposit of Rs. 80,000 (two months' rent) is payable on signing. Within 7 days of the Tenant vacating, the Landlord shall return the deposit together with an itemised statement of any deductions, supported by bills or receipts.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers at any time during the tenancy, and the Tenant shall not object to such visits.

8. The Tenant shall bear the cost of periodic servicing and repair of all appliances provided by the Landlord, including air conditioners, geysers, water purifiers and the refrigerator.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Kerala.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
