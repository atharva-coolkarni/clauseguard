RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Noida on 19 March 2026 between Venkat Das, residing at 30, MG Road, Sector 50, Noida (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Nikhil Chatterjee, presently residing at 1, Temple Street, Vyttila, Kochi (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 1446, Shanti Enclave, Sector 137, Noida (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of twelve months commencing on 1 April 2026 and ending on 31 March 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 29,500, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Landlord shall have the right to recover from the security deposit of Rs. 59,000 such sums as he considers proper for repairs, cleaning, painting or replacement of any items, without being obliged to show any bill or receipt to the Tenant.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. Throughout the tenancy, the Landlord shall be entitled to show the Premises to anyone interested in renting or buying it, at any time, and the Tenant shall raise no objection.

8. When the Tenant moves out, Rs. 15,000 shall be deducted from the security deposit as a fixed charge for painting and cleaning, irrespective of whether such work is actually needed.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Noida.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
