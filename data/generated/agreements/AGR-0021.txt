RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Hyderabad, Telangana on 16 November 2026 by and between Neha Patel (the "Landlord") of the one part and Fatima Das (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. D-902 in Shanti Enclave, Kukatpally, Hyderabad - 500061, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 December 2026 and ending on 31 October 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 34,500, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 69,000 may be adjusted only against (a) unpaid rent, (b) unpaid electricity, water or maintenance bills, and (c) the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Both parties shall record the condition of the Premises with dated photographs at move-in and move-out.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. Except in an emergency, the Landlord shall give at least 24 hours' notice before entering the Premises. In an emergency such as fire, gas leakage or major water leakage, the Landlord may enter without notice and shall inform the Tenant immediately.

8. Normal wear and tear arising from ordinary residential use, including fading or minor marks on painted walls, shall not be treated as damage and shall not be charged to the Tenant at the end of the tenancy.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Telangana.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
