RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Pune on 14 February 2026 between Divya Nair, residing at 39, Main Road, Kothrud, Pune (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Karthik Khan, presently residing at 121, Main Road, Goregaon East, Mumbai (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 1495 in Ganga Residency, Kothrud, Pune - 411023, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 March 2026 to 31 January 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 56,500, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 1,13,000 shall be refunded to the Tenant at the sole discretion of the Landlord after the Tenant vacates the Premises, subject to such deductions as the Landlord may consider appropriate.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Landlord from entering the Premises without notice where it is necessary to prevent immediate danger to life or serious damage to the building, provided the Tenant is informed at once.

8. The Tenant shall return the Premises in a reasonably clean condition, similar to its condition at the start of the tenancy, normal wear and tear excepted. The Tenant shall not be required to pay for professional deep cleaning or repainting.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Pune.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
