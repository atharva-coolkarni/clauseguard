RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Noida, Uttar Pradesh on 17 April 2026 by and between Ayesha Nair (the "Landlord") of the one part and Shreya Nair (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 665 in Ganga Residency, Sector 137, Noida - 201050, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 May 2026 and ending on 31 March 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 34,000, payable in advance on or before the 5th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 68,000 may be adjusted only against (a) unpaid rent, (b) unpaid electricity, water or maintenance bills, and (c) the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Both parties shall record the condition of the Premises with dated photographs at move-in and move-out.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

8. Normal wear and tear arising from ordinary residential use, including fading or minor marks on painted walls, shall not be treated as damage and shall not be charged to the Tenant at the end of the tenancy.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Uttar Pradesh.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
