RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Ahmedabad on 23 December 2025 between Imran Verma, residing at 169, Church Street, Vastrapur, Ahmedabad (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Harpreet Patel, presently residing at 226, Temple Street, Palarivattom, Kochi (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 3 BHK flat No. B-1202 in Ganga Residency, Maninagar, Ahmedabad - 380075, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 January 2026 to 30 November 2026.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 43,000, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. Any rent or utility charges remaining unpaid when the Tenant vacates, and the cost of making good any damage caused by the Tenant, may be recovered by the Landlord out of the security deposit of Rs. 86,000, provided that the Landlord first shows the Tenant the relevant bills.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. When moving out, the Tenant shall clear out all personal belongings and waste, sweep the Premises clean and hand over all keys to the Landlord.

8. In the final month of the tenancy, the Landlord may bring prospective tenants to view the Premises, giving at least 24 hours' notice and at times agreed with the Tenant.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Ahmedabad.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
