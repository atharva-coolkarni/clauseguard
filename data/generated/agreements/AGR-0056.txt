RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Hyderabad, Telangana on 14 February 2026 by and between Sanjay Khan (the "Landlord") of the one part and Nikhil Fernandes (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. D-203, Lakeview Towers, Kondapur, Hyderabad (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 March 2026 and ending on 31 January 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 9,000, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 45,000, equal to five months' rent, before taking possession of the Premises. The deposit shall be refunded without interest when the Tenant vacates.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. All repairs of whatever nature, including structural repairs, roof leakage, seepage and plumbing work inside the walls, shall be carried out by the Tenant at the Tenant's own cost during the tenancy.

8. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Landlord from entering the Premises without notice where it is necessary to prevent immediate danger to life or serious damage to the building, provided the Tenant is informed at once.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Telangana.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
