RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Bengaluru, Karnataka on 21 March 2026 by and between Lakshmi Gupta (the "Landlord") of the one part and Manoj Shetty (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 1121, Orchid Court, Indiranagar, Bengaluru (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 April 2026 and ending on 28 February 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 60,000, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Landlord shall be entitled to deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 1,20,000 any rent or utility dues outstanding on the date of vacating, and the cost of repairing any damage caused by the Tenant, as shown by bills which the Landlord shall share with the Tenant before making the deduction.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. During the last month of the tenancy, the Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants after giving 24 hours' notice, at times mutually convenient to both parties.

8. The Tenant shall bear the cost of repairing any damage to the Premises or its fittings caused by the Tenant, members of the Tenant's family or the Tenant's guests, other than normal wear and tear.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Karnataka.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
