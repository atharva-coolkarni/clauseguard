RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Bengaluru, Karnataka on 18 February 2026 by and between Aditya Nair (the "Landlord") of the one part and Swati Menon (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. D-801, Lakeview Towers, Whitefield, Bengaluru (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 March 2026 and ending on 31 January 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 26,000 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Landlord shall be entitled to deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 52,000 any rent or utility dues outstanding on the date of vacating, and the cost of repairing any damage caused by the Tenant, as shown by bills which the Landlord shall share with the Tenant before making the deduction.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

8. The Tenant shall return the Premises in a reasonably clean condition, similar to its condition at the start of the tenancy, normal wear and tear excepted. The Tenant shall not be required to pay for professional deep cleaning or repainting.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Karnataka.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
