RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Kochi on 29 May 2026 between Rahul Joshi, residing at 217, MG Road, Palarivattom, Kochi (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Harpreet Rao, presently residing at 198, Church Street, Jayanagar, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. B-402 in Ganga Residency, Edappally, Kochi - 682044, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 June 2026 and ending on 30 April 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 19,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. If the Tenant vacates the Premises before the expiry of the term for any reason whatsoever, including after giving notice, the entire security deposit of Rs. 39,000 shall stand forfeited to the Landlord.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. On vacating, the Tenant shall remove all personal belongings and rubbish and leave the Premises broom-clean, with all keys returned to the Landlord.

8. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Landlord from entering the Premises without notice where it is necessary to prevent immediate danger to life or serious damage to the building, provided the Tenant is informed at once.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Kochi.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
