RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Bengaluru on 22 June 2026 between Ravi Banerjee, residing at 197, Station Road, Whitefield, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Ayesha Iyer, presently residing at 96, Main Road, Kukatpally, Hyderabad (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 688 in Sunrise Apartments, Koramangala, Bengaluru - 560070, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 July 2026 and ending on 31 May 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 52,500 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Landlord shall be entitled to deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 1,05,000 any rent or utility dues outstanding on the date of vacating, and the cost of repairing any damage caused by the Tenant, as shown by bills which the Landlord shall share with the Tenant before making the deduction.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. The Tenant shall inform the Landlord in writing of any repair that is the Landlord's responsibility. If the Landlord does not carry out the repair within 15 days of such notice, the Tenant may get it done and adjust the reasonable cost, supported by receipts, against the next month's rent.

8. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Landlord from entering the Premises without notice where it is necessary to prevent immediate danger to life or serious damage to the building, provided the Tenant is informed at once.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Bengaluru.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
