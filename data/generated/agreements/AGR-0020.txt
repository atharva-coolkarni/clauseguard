RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Pune, Maharashtra on 23 October 2026 by and between Aditya Joshi (the "Landlord") of the one part and Pooja Pillai (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 3 BHK flat No. A-603 in Ganga Residency, Aundh, Pune - 411005, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of twelve months commencing on 1 November 2026 and ending on 31 October 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 60,000 on or before the 5th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. Out of the security deposit of Rs. 1,20,000, an amount equal to one month's rent shall be non-refundable and shall be retained by the Landlord towards maintenance charges, irrespective of the condition of the Premises at the time of vacating.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. The Landlord shall retain a duplicate key to the Premises and may enter the Premises in the absence of the Tenant whenever he considers it necessary.

8. The Tenant shall inform the Landlord in writing of any repair that is the Landlord's responsibility. If the Landlord does not carry out the repair within 7 days of such notice, the Tenant may get it done and adjust the reasonable cost, supported by receipts, against the next month's rent.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Pune.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
