RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Kochi on 25 August 2026 between Lakshmi Kulkarni, residing at 27, Park Avenue, Edappally, Kochi (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Deepa Mehta, presently residing at 77, MG Road, Maninagar, Ahmedabad (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. A-1304, Orchid Court, Kakkanad, Kochi (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 September 2026 and ending on 31 July 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 44,500, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Landlord shall be entitled to deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 89,000 any rent or utility dues outstanding on the date of vacating, and the cost of repairing any damage caused by the Tenant, as shown by bills which the Landlord shall share with the Tenant before making the deduction.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

8. The Tenant shall return the Premises in a reasonably clean condition, similar to its condition at the start of the tenancy, normal wear and tear excepted. The Tenant shall not be required to pay for professional deep cleaning or repainting.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Kerala.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
