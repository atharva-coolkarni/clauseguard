RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at New Delhi, Delhi on 29 October 2026 by and between Venkat Joshi (the "Landlord") of the one part and Suresh Reddy (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. A-304 in Lakeview Towers, Mayur Vihar, New Delhi - 110096, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is twelve months, from 1 November 2026 to 31 October 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 31,000 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 62,000 may be adjusted only against (a) unpaid rent, (b) unpaid electricity, water or maintenance bills, and (c) the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Both parties shall record the condition of the Premises with dated photographs at move-in and move-out.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. The Landlord or his authorised agents shall have the right to enter the Premises at any time, with or without notice, for the purpose of inspection.

8. A fixed amount of Rs. 20,000 towards painting and deep cleaning shall be deducted from the security deposit when the Tenant vacates, whether or not any painting or cleaning is required.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Delhi.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
