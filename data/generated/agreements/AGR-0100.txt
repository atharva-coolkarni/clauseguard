RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Mumbai on 19 September 2026 between Vikram Patel, residing at 166, Main Road, Andheri West, Mumbai (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Ananya Kulkarni, presently residing at 63, MG Road, New Town, Kolkata (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. A-804, Orchid Court, Powai, Mumbai (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 October 2026 and ending on 31 August 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 50,000 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. Out of the security deposit of Rs. 1,00,000, an amount equal to one month's rent shall be non-refundable and shall be retained by the Landlord towards maintenance charges, irrespective of the condition of the Premises at the time of vacating.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. Normal wear and tear arising from ordinary residential use, including fading or minor marks on painted walls, shall not be treated as damage and shall not be charged to the Tenant at the end of the tenancy.

8. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Landlord from entering the Premises without notice where it is necessary to prevent immediate danger to life or serious damage to the building, provided the Tenant is informed at once.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Mumbai.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
