RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Kochi, Kerala on 28 August 2026 by and between Venkat Banerjee (the "Landlord") of the one part and Karthik Rao (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. A-603, Sunrise Apartments, Vyttila, Kochi (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 September 2026 to 31 July 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 46,000, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Tenant shall keep with the Landlord a refundable deposit of Rs. 92,000, being two months' rent. The Landlord shall repay it within 15 days after the Tenant moves out, and shall give the Tenant a written list of any amounts deducted along with the supporting bills.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Landlord may enter the Premises without notice only where this is needed to prevent an immediate risk to life or serious damage to the building, and shall inform the Tenant straight away.

8. When the Tenant moves out, Rs. 8,000 shall be deducted from the security deposit as a fixed charge for painting and cleaning, irrespective of whether such work is actually needed.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Kochi.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
