RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Noida, Uttar Pradesh on 28 March 2026 by and between Sanjay Gupta (the "Landlord") of the one part and Rahul Shetty (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 749, Silver Oak Apartments, Sector 137, Noida (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 April 2026 and ending on 28 February 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 13,000 on or before the 5th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 1,30,000, equal to ten months' rent, before taking possession of the Premises. The deposit shall be refunded without interest when the Tenant vacates.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. The Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers at any time during the tenancy, and the Tenant shall not object to such visits.

8. At the time of vacating the Premises, the Tenant shall get the entire Premises repainted at the Tenant's own cost, irrespective of the condition of the walls or the period of occupation.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Uttar Pradesh.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
