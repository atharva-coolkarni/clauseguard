RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Mumbai, Maharashtra on 23 June 2026 by and between Suresh Pillai (the "Landlord") of the one part and Karthik Iyer (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 1381 in Green Valley Residency, Goregaon East, Mumbai - 400062, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 July 2026 to 31 May 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 34,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. If the Tenant vacates the Premises before the expiry of the term for any reason whatsoever, including after giving notice, the entire security deposit of Rs. 69,000 shall stand forfeited to the Landlord.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. The Tenant shall hand over the Premises in brand-new condition, failing which the Landlord shall be entitled to recover the cost of restoring the Premises to such condition from the Tenant.

8. During the last month of the tenancy, the Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants after giving 24 hours' notice, at times mutually convenient to both parties.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Maharashtra.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
