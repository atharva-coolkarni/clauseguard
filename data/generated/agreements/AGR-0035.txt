RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Ahmedabad, Gujarat on 28 July 2026 by and between Nikhil Banerjee (the "Landlord") of the one part and Meera Verma (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. B-1402, Silver Oak Apartments, Maninagar, Ahmedabad (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 August 2026 and ending on 30 June 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 57,500, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. A refundable security deposit of Rs. 1,15,000 (two months' rent) is payable on signing. Within 7 days of the Tenant vacating, the Landlord shall return the deposit together with an itemised statement of any deductions, supported by bills or receipts.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, whitewashing of walls and painting of doors and windows. The Tenant shall attend to minor day-to-day repairs such as replacement of switches and sockets, repair of taps and kitchen fixtures, and cleaning of drains.

8. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Landlord from entering the Premises without notice where it is necessary to prevent immediate danger to life or serious damage to the building, provided the Tenant is informed at once.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Gujarat.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
