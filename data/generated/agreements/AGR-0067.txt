RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Pune on 22 August 2026 between Divya Khan, residing at 201, Station Road, Viman Nagar, Pune (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Neha Iyer, presently residing at 99, MG Road, Powai, Mumbai (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 1 BHK flat No. 903 in Maple Heights, Kothrud, Pune - 411085, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 September 2026 to 31 July 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 57,500 on or before the 10th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. If the Tenant vacates the Premises before the expiry of the term for any reason whatsoever, including after giving notice, the entire security deposit of Rs. 1,15,000 shall stand forfeited to the Landlord.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, whitewashing of walls and painting of doors and windows. The Tenant shall attend to minor day-to-day repairs such as replacement of switches and sockets, repair of taps and kitchen fixtures, and cleaning of drains.

8. The Landlord or his authorised agents shall have the right to enter the Premises at any time, with or without notice, for the purpose of inspection.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Pune.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
