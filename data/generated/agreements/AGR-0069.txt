RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at New Delhi, Delhi on 24 May 2026 by and between Harpreet Chatterjee (the "Landlord") of the one part and Anil Khan (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. A-604, Green Valley Residency, Saket, New Delhi (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 June 2026 to 30 April 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 55,500 on or before the 5th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Tenant has paid the Landlord an interest-free, refundable security deposit of Rs. 55,500, equal to one month's rent. The Landlord shall refund the deposit on the date the Tenant hands over vacant possession of the Premises, after deducting only rent or utility charges that remain unpaid on that date.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, whitewashing of walls and painting of doors and windows. The Tenant shall attend to minor day-to-day repairs such as replacement of switches and sockets, repair of taps and kitchen fixtures, and cleaning of drains.

8. The Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers at any time during the tenancy, and the Tenant shall not object to such visits.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at New Delhi.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
