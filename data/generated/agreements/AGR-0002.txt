RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Noida on 20 December 2025 between Nikhil Rao, residing at 95, Temple Street, Sector 50, Noida (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Ayesha Banerjee, presently residing at 172, Temple Street, Madhapur, Hyderabad (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 1029 in Shanti Enclave, Sector 137, Noida - 201011, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 January 2026 to 30 November 2026.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 57,000, payable in advance on or before the 10th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Tenant shall keep with the Landlord a refundable deposit of Rs. 1,14,000, being two months' rent. The Landlord shall repay it within 7 days after the Tenant moves out, and shall give the Tenant a written list of any amounts deducted along with the supporting bills.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

7. Any damage to the Premises or its fittings caused by the Tenant, the Tenant's family members or visitors shall be repaired at the Tenant's cost, except ordinary wear and tear.

8. The Landlord shall keep a spare key to the Premises and shall be free to enter the Premises when the Tenant is not present, whenever he thinks fit.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Uttar Pradesh.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
