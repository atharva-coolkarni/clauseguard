RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Kolkata, West Bengal on 17 September 2026 by and between Meera Nair (the "Landlord") of the one part and Shreya Verma (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 3 BHK flat No. 242 in Ganga Residency, Garia, Kolkata - 700004, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 October 2026 to 31 August 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 51,500 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 1,03,000 may be adjusted only against (a) unpaid rent, (b) unpaid electricity, water or maintenance bills, and (c) the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Both parties shall record the condition of the Premises with dated photographs at move-in and move-out.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. During the last month of the tenancy, the Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants after giving 24 hours' notice, at times mutually convenient to both parties.

8. Before vacating the Premises, the Tenant shall, at the Tenant's own cost, get the entire Premises professionally deep cleaned and repainted through a vendor approved by the Landlord, irrespective of the condition of the Premises, failing which the cost shall be recovered from the security deposit.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in West Bengal.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
