RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Pune on 22 October 2026 between Karthik Fernandes, residing at 190, Main Road, Aundh, Pune (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Suresh Kulkarni, presently residing at 30, Park Avenue, Madhapur, Hyderabad (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. C-102 in Shanti Enclave, Kothrud, Pune - 411046, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 November 2026 and ending on 30 September 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 16,000 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 32,000 may be adjusted only against (a) unpaid rent, (b) unpaid electricity, water or maintenance bills, and (c) the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Both parties shall record the condition of the Premises with dated photographs at move-in and move-out.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. The Tenant shall bear the cost of repairing any damage to the Premises or its fittings caused by the Tenant, members of the Tenant's family or the Tenant's guests, other than normal wear and tear.

8. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Pune.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
