RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at New Delhi, Delhi on 25 February 2026 by and between Joseph Gupta (the "Landlord") of the one part and Sneha Pillai (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. 252, Sunrise Apartments, Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is twelve months, from 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 12,500 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. The Landlord shall be entitled to deduct from the security deposit of Rs. 25,000 any rent or utility dues outstanding on the date of vacating, and the cost of repairing any damage caused by the Tenant, as shown by bills which the Landlord shall share with the Tenant before making the deduction.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. A fixed amount of Rs. 20,000 towards painting and deep cleaning shall be deducted from the security deposit when the Tenant vacates, whether or not any painting or cleaning is required.

8. During the last month of the tenancy, the Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants after giving 24 hours' notice, at times mutually convenient to both parties.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at New Delhi.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
