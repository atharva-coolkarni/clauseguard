RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Chennai on 21 January 2026 between Manoj Gupta, residing at 28, Park Avenue, Velachery, Chennai (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Manoj Fernandes, presently residing at 121, MG Road, Garia, Kolkata (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 1298 in Green Valley Residency, T. Nagar, Chennai - 600069, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is twelve months, from 1 February 2026 to 31 January 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 8,500, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 25,500, equal to three months' rent, before taking possession of the Premises. The deposit shall be refunded without interest when the Tenant vacates.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

7. The Landlord shall retain a duplicate key to the Premises and may enter the Premises in the absence of the Tenant whenever he considers it necessary.

8. Normal wear and tear arising from ordinary residential use, including fading or minor marks on painted walls, shall not be treated as damage and shall not be charged to the Tenant at the end of the tenancy.

9. This Agreement may be ended by either the Landlord or the Tenant on one month's written notice to the other party.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the tenancy laws in force in Tamil Nadu.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
