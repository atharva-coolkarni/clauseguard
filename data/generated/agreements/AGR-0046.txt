RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Mumbai, Maharashtra on 28 November 2026 by and between Joseph Das (the "Landlord") of the one part and Divya Das (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 3 BHK flat No. C-903 in Ganga Residency, Chembur, Mumbai - 400041, along with one covered parking space.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 December 2026 to 31 October 2027.

3. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 52,000 on or before the 7th day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

4. If the Tenant vacates the Premises before the expiry of the term for any reason whatsoever, including after giving notice, the entire security deposit of Rs. 1,04,000 shall stand forfeited to the Landlord.

5. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. The Landlord shall retain a duplicate key to the Premises and may enter the Premises in the absence of the Tenant whenever he considers it necessary.

8. The Tenant shall bear the cost of periodic servicing and repair of all appliances provided by the Landlord, including air conditioners, geysers, water purifiers and the refrigerator.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Mumbai.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
