RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made at Mumbai on 23 October 2026 between Neha Menon, residing at 185, MG Road, Chembur, Mumbai (hereinafter called the "Landlord"), and Manoj Khan, presently residing at 76, Temple Street, Rohini, New Delhi (hereinafter called the "Tenant").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 2 BHK flat No. 1453 in Shanti Enclave, Powai, Mumbai - 400067, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 November 2026 and ending on 30 September 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 20,000, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The security deposit of Rs. 40,000 may be adjusted only against (a) unpaid rent, (b) unpaid electricity, water or maintenance bills, and (c) the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Both parties shall record the condition of the Premises with dated photographs at move-in and move-out.

5. The Tenant shall pay electricity charges as per the separate meter installed in the Premises and water charges as billed by the society, directly to the authorities concerned.

6. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

7. The Tenant shall hand over the Premises in brand-new condition, failing which the Landlord shall be entitled to recover the cost of restoring the Premises to such condition from the Tenant.

8. The Landlord may inspect the Premises at any time after informing the Tenant by phone or message at least 4 hour(s) in advance.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Mumbai.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
