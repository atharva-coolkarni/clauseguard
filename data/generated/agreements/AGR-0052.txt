RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Kolkata, West Bengal on 18 February 2026 by and between Anil Chatterjee (the "Landlord") of the one part and Aditya Joshi (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Landlord agrees to let out the residential flat bearing No. B-1402, Ganga Residency, New Town, Kolkata (the "Premises"), together with the fittings listed in the Schedule, to the Tenant.

2. The term of this Agreement is eleven months, from 1 March 2026 to 31 January 2027.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 32,000, payable in advance on or before the 5th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. Out of the security deposit of Rs. 64,000, an amount equal to one month's rent shall be non-refundable and shall be retained by the Landlord towards maintenance charges, irrespective of the condition of the Premises at the time of vacating.

5. The Tenant shall use the Premises for residence only and shall follow the bye-laws of the housing society.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. The Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs or replacement after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' prior written notice, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m.

8. Normal wear and tear arising from ordinary residential use, including fading or minor marks on painted walls, shall not be treated as damage and shall not be charged to the Tenant at the end of the tenancy.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The cost of stamp paper and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Kolkata.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
