RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT OF TENANCY is executed at Pune, Maharashtra on 29 August 2026 by and between Karthik Gupta (the "Landlord") of the one part and Joseph Singh (the "Tenant") of the other part.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Premises let out under this Agreement is the 3 BHK flat No. 260 in Sunrise Apartments, Viman Nagar, Pune - 411095, along with one covered parking space.

2. This tenancy shall be for a period of eleven months commencing on 1 September 2026 and ending on 31 July 2027, and may be renewed on mutually agreed terms.

3. The monthly rent for the Premises is Rs. 12,500, payable in advance on or before the 7th of every month. The Landlord shall issue a receipt for every payment.

4. The Tenant shall keep with the Landlord a refundable deposit of Rs. 25,000, being two months' rent. The Landlord shall repay it within 15 days after the Tenant moves out, and shall give the Tenant a written list of any amounts deducted along with the supporting bills.

5. The Premises shall be used by the Tenant only for residential purposes for the Tenant and the Tenant's family.

6. Electricity, piped gas and internet charges for the period of the tenancy shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual consumption.

7. When moving out, the Tenant shall clear out all personal belongings and waste, sweep the Premises clean and hand over all keys to the Landlord.

8. A phone call or text message to the Tenant at least 1 hour(s) beforehand shall be sufficient notice for the Landlord to inspect the Premises at any time.

9. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other one month's notice in writing.

10. The stamp duty and registration charges for this Agreement shall be shared equally by the Landlord and the Tenant.

11. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate authority or court at Pune.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first mentioned above.
